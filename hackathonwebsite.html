Before you sign up.

Short answers to what students and families ask most.

Who can join?

Middle and high school students. The program focuses on students from underrepresented groups, and the hackathon is open to any middle or high schooler. Each session takes 30 participants.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not for stage 01. It starts from your first line of Python.

Can I skip ahead?

Yes, if you already know the earlier material. Stages 02 and 03 run a 2-day intro for students joining for the first time.

What do I take home?

Projects you built, mentor feedback, demo experience and a certificate.
